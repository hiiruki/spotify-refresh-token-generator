Per Spotify Official Redirect URI Docs:
  • HTTPS Required for all public URLs (e.g. https://example.com/callback).
  • Explicit Loopback: Must use explicit IPv4 (http://127.0.0.1:3000/callback) or IPv6 (http://[::1]:3000/callback).
  • localhost is strictly NOT allowed by Spotify anymore.
  • Dynamic ports are permitted for loopback IP literals (IETF RFC 8252).

How to Get Spotify Client ID & Secret

  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Click the Create app button in the top right corner.
  3. Fill in the App name (e.g. My Bot) and App description.
  4. Under the Redirect URIs section, add the following callback URL:
    http://127.0.0.1:3000/callback
    (Or your production Cloudflare Pages URL when deployed)
  5. Select the API you wish to use (check Web API), accept the terms of service, and click Save.
  6. Once created, open your app's Settings tab.
  7. Copy your Client ID, then click View client secret to copy your Client Secret.
  8. Paste your Client ID and Client Secret into the generator form, select your desired scopes, and click Generate Refresh Token.
